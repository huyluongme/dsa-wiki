# DSA Wiki

A wiki for learning and quick reference on data structures and algorithms.

Use it in two ways:

- **Learn** – read the pages in order, from the basics to more advanced topics.
- **Look up** – press the search box and jump straight to the topic you forgot.

## How the pages are organized

Every topic follows the same layout, so you always know where to look:

1. **Idea** – what it is and why it exists.
2. **How it works** – the steps, usually with a diagram.
3. **Code** – implementations you can switch between languages.
4. **Complexity** – time and space cost.
5. **When to use it** – typical applications and pitfalls.
